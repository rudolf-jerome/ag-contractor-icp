# Ag Contractor ICP and Lead Sources

## What Harvest Yield does
Job tracking, machine monitoring, field mapping, time tracking and invoicing software tailored to agricultural contractors and custom applicators.

## Ideal customer profile (US)
- **Who:** Custom harvesters, custom applicators, and turnkey agricultural contracting businesses
- **Signals:** multi-machine fleets, multiple operators, extensive acreage across several clients, multi-state routes
- **Pain:** paper job sheets, slow invoicing, disputed acreage, poor visibility into machine and fuel costs

## Lead sources
| Organization | Target profile | Prospecting value |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. Custom Harvesters, Inc. (USCHI) | Custom combine and silage harvesting contractors | Public directory of 450+ member harvest crews operating nationwide |
| Agricultural Retailers Association (ARA) | Commercial custom fertilizer and chemical applicators | Reaches retail agronomists and spray contractors with large applicators |
| National Agricultural Aviation Association (NAAA) | Aerial custom applicators (crop dusters and drones) | High-volume job logging and precise acre tracking |

> Don't commit prospect contact details (names, emails, phones) to this repo. Keep them in the CRM.
